# Privacy and safety requirements

- Collect the minimum data required.
- Do not request bank login credentials, PINs or OTPs.
- Do not initiate transfers.
- Do not claim settlement verification from OCR.
- Do not store uploaded images by default.
- Use synthetic transaction samples for development wherever possible.
- Obtain informed consent for participant data and banknote images.
- Keep personal identifiers out of model datasets and logs.
- Define deletion and backup retention before enabling cloud accounts.
- Restrict and document access to any research dataset.

This document is a design baseline, not legal advice or a completed privacy assessment.
